About DailyTools Hub

We build small, honest tools that solve everyday problems in seconds.

DailyTools Hub started with a simple frustration: needing to calculate an EMI, check a GST amount, or convert a date, and having to wade through cluttered, ad-heavy websites to do it. We wanted a place where the tool loads instantly, does exactly one job well, and gets out of your way.

What we believe

  • Tools should work instantly, without sign-up or accounts.
  • Your data belongs to you — most calculations happen entirely in your browser.
  • A website can be fast, useful, and still look good.
  • Free tools should be funded honestly, through non-intrusive advertising — never through selling your data.

What we offer today

15 tools across finance, date & time, conversions, text, security and file handling — from an Age Calculator to an Image Compressor. We're actively building more based on what people search for and ask us about.

Who's behind it

DailyTools Hub is an independent project built and maintained by a small team who use these tools ourselves, every week. We keep the codebase simple on purpose, so we can fix bugs fast and ship new tools regularly.
