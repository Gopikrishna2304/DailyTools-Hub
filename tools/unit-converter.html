What is this tool?

The Unit Converter converts values between common units of length, weight, area, speed, and volume — such as kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, and meters to feet — using precise standard conversion factors.

How to use it

  1. Select a category (length, weight, area, speed, or volume).
  2. Enter the value and choose the unit you're converting from.
  3. Choose the unit you want to convert to — the result updates instantly.

Example

Converting 10 kilometers to miles gives approximately 6.21 miles. Converting 5 kilograms to pounds gives approximately 11.02 pounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the conversions? +
Conversions use standard, internationally recognized conversion factors and are accurate to several decimal places.
Can I convert between different categories, like weight to length? +
No — units can only be converted within the same category, since units like kilograms and meters measure different physical quantities.
