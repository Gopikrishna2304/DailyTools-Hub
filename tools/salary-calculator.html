What is this tool?

The Salary Calculator converts a monthly salary figure into its annual equivalent, and applies a deduction percentage you provide (to represent tax, provident fund, insurance, etc.) to estimate net take-home pay.

How to use it

  1. Enter your gross monthly salary.
  2. Enter an estimated combined deduction percentage — or leave it blank if you just want the annual conversion.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see your gross annual salary and estimated net figures.

Example

A monthly salary of ₹60,000 works out to a gross annual salary of ₹7,20,000. With an estimated 10% combined deduction, the estimated net annual salary is ₹6,48,000 (₹54,000/month).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official tax calculation? +
No. This tool applies a single deduction percentage that you provide as a simplified estimate. It does not calculate official income tax slabs, PF rules, or other statutory deductions for any specific country. For accurate figures, consult your payroll department or a tax professional.
What should I enter for deductions if I don't know the exact number? +
You can leave it blank to see just the gross annual conversion, or enter a rough estimate (e.g. 10-20%) to get a ballpark net figure.
