What is this tool?

The Character Counter gives an instant, live count of characters (with and without spaces), words, and lines in any block of text — useful when writing to a strict character limit, like a tweet, meta description, or bio.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Watch the counts update live as you type.

Example

A typical tweet-length message of 280 characters will show a word count around 45-55 words, depending on average word length.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the character count include spaces? +
"Total Characters" includes spaces; "Chars (no spaces)" excludes them, so you can check both depending on the limit you're working with.
How are lines counted? +
Each line break (pressing Enter) counts as a new line, including empty lines.
