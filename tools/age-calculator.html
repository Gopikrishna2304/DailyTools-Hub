What is this tool?

The Age Calculator works out your exact age — down to the year, month and day — from your date of birth. It's useful for form filling, eligibility checks, birthday planning, or simple curiosity.

How to use it

  1. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
  2. (Optional) Choose a different reference date if you want your age as of a specific day, instead of today.
  3. Click "Calculate Age" to see your age in years, months and days, plus days left until your next birthday.

Example

If you were born on 15 August 2000 and calculate your age today, the tool shows your age in complete years, the extra months since your last birthday, and the extra days since your last month-anniversary — along with how many days remain until 15 August of next year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated? +
Age is calculated by finding the difference between your date of birth and today's date (or any date you choose), expressed in complete years, months and days.
Why does my age in months look different from what I expected? +
The calculator counts only complete months. If your birth day hasn't occurred yet in the current month, that month isn't counted as complete.
Is my date of birth stored anywhere? +
No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers or saved.
