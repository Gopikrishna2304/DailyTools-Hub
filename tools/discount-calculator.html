What is this tool?

The Discount Calculator quickly works out the final price and total savings when a percentage discount is applied to an item's original price — handy for shopping, sales, and comparing deals.

How to use it

  1. Enter the original price of the item.
  2. Enter the discount percentage being offered.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the final price and how much you save.

Example

An item priced at ₹2,000 with a 25% discount costs ₹1,500 after the discount, saving you ₹500.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the discount amount calculated? +
Discount amount = Original price × (Discount % ÷ 100). The final price is the original price minus the discount amount.
Does this include tax? +
No — this calculates the discount only. Use the GST Calculator afterward if you need to add tax to the discounted price.
