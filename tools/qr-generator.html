What is this tool?

This tool creates a QR code — a scannable square barcode — from text, a website link, or contact details, so you can share information quickly by letting someone scan it with their phone camera.

How to use it

  1. Choose the content type: plain text/URL, or contact info.
  2. Fill in the relevant fields.
  3. Click "Generate QR Code", then "Download QR Code" to save it as an image.

Example

Paste your website URL, click generate, and you'll get a scannable code that opens your site when scanned with any phone camera.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data stored when I generate a QR code? +
No — the QR code is drawn entirely in your browser. We don't store or transmit the content you enter.
What format is the downloaded QR code? +
The QR code downloads as a PNG image, suitable for printing or sharing digitally.
