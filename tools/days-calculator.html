What is this tool?

The Days Calculator answers three common questions: how many days lie between two dates, how many days remain until an upcoming date, and how many days have passed since a past date — useful for deadlines, countdowns, and anniversaries.

How to use it

  1. Choose the mode that matches your question.
  2. Enter the required date(s).
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the day count.

Example

If today is 13 August 2026 and your target date is 25 December 2026, "Days until a future date" shows how many days remain until Christmas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between this and the Date Calculator? +
This tool focuses purely on day counts (between dates, or from today). The Date Calculator additionally breaks results into years/months/days and can add or subtract days from a date.
Does "days until" count today? +
No — it shows the number of full days between today and the target date, so the count is 0 if the target date is today.
