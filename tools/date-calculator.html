What is this tool?

The Date Calculator has three modes: finding how many years, months and days lie between two dates; adding a number of days to a date to find a future date; or subtracting days to find a past date.

How to use it

  1. Pick a mode from the dropdown.
  2. Fill in the date(s) requested.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the result instantly.

Example

Finding the difference between 1 January 2026 and 15 August 2026 shows the exact number of months and days between them, and the total day count. Adding 45 days to 1 January 2026 gives 15 February 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the date difference include both start and end dates? +
The difference is calculated as the number of days between the two dates (end minus start), not an inclusive day count.
Can I subtract more days than are in the month? +
Yes — the calculator correctly rolls over into previous or following months and years as needed.
