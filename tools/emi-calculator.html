What is this tool?

The EMI Calculator computes your fixed monthly repayment amount for a loan, based on the principal, annual interest rate, and repayment tenure — using the standard reducing-balance formula banks use.

How to use it

  1. Enter the total loan amount you plan to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate offered by your lender.
  3. Enter the loan tenure in months (e.g. 5 years = 60 months).
  4. Click "Calculate EMI" to see your monthly payment, total interest, and total amount payable.

Example

For a loan of ₹5,00,000 at 8.5% annual interest over 60 months, the calculator shows the fixed monthly EMI along with how much of the total payment goes toward interest versus principal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI? +
EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed payment made every month toward repaying a loan, covering both principal and interest.
How is EMI calculated? +
EMI is calculated using the loan amount, the monthly interest rate, and the number of monthly installments, using the standard reducing-balance EMI formula.
Does this account for processing fees or prepayment charges? +
No — this is a straightforward EMI estimate based on principal, rate and tenure only. Always check your loan agreement for fees, insurance, and prepayment terms with your lender.
