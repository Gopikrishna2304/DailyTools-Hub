What is this tool?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is added to the price of most goods and services. This calculator lets you either add GST on top of a base price, or work backward from a GST-inclusive price to find the original amount and the tax portion.

How to use it

  1. Choose whether you want to add GST or remove GST.
  2. Enter the amount and select the applicable GST rate.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the original amount, GST amount, and final amount.

Example

Adding 18% GST to ₹1,000 gives a GST amount of ₹180 and a final amount of ₹1,180. Removing 18% GST from a final amount of ₹1,180 gives back an original amount of ₹1,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between adding and removing GST? +
"Add GST" starts from a price before tax and calculates the tax-inclusive total. "Remove GST" starts from a tax-inclusive price and works backward to find the pre-tax amount.
Which GST rate should I use? +
GST rates vary by category of goods or service. Check the applicable rate for your specific product or service with official GST rate schedules or your accountant.
