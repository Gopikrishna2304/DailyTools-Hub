What is this tool?

The Word Counter gives a live breakdown of your text — total words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and an estimated reading time — useful for essays, social posts, and SEO content.

How to use it

  1. Type directly into the text box, or paste text you already have.
  2. Watch the stats update automatically as you type.

Example

Pasting a 500-word article shows roughly 2,800 characters, about 25 sentences, and an estimated reading time of around 2-3 minutes at average reading speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated? +
Reading time is estimated using an average reading speed of about 200 words per minute.
Is my text saved or sent anywhere? +
No. All counting happens locally in your browser — your text is never uploaded or stored.
