Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

DailyTools Hub ("we", "our", "us") provides free online tools. This policy explains what information we collect and how it's used.

1. Data processed by our tools

Most calculators and converters on this site (Age Calculator, EMI Calculator, GST Calculator, Unit Converter, Word Counter, Password Generator, QR Code Generator, Image Compressor, and others) run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The numbers, text, or images you enter are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers, unless a specific tool clearly states otherwise on its own page.

2. Information we collect automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics providers may automatically log standard technical information such as your approximate location (country/region), browser type, device type, and pages visited. This is used only in aggregate to understand traffic and improve the site — not to identify you personally.

3. Local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and your recently used tools. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

4. Cookies and analytics

If we enable a privacy-conscious analytics service, it may use cookies or similar technology to count visits and popular pages. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles of individual users ourselves.

5. Advertising

If and when we display ads through third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense), those networks may use cookies or device identifiers to serve relevant ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies. We will disclose our ad partners here once ads are active.

6. Third-party services

We may load fonts, icons, or scripts from trusted content delivery networks (CDNs). These providers may log requests (such as IP address) as part of normal web infrastructure operation.

7. Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove saved preferences and recently used tools. You can also use browser settings or extensions to block cookies and trackers.

8. Children's privacy

DailyTools Hub is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our Contact page.
